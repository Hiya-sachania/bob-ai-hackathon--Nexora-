What we built

A rule-based monitor that compares every patient visit against the trial protocol, classifies deviations by ICH E6 GCP severity, and scores every site's risk in real time.

1

Detect

Compares patient records against protocol specs — visit windows, required assessments, dosing, eligibility, and co-medications — to catch every type of deviation.

2

Classify

Labels every deviation Major, Minor, or Administrative — following the real ICH E6(R2) GCP standard. Completely missed visits are detected and flagged separately.

3

Score & report

Ranks every site by weighted risk score (0–100) and generates a CAPA-ready report with corrective and preventive actions for every finding.
